About

Dr. Huizhen Jia is an associate professor and master’s supervisor at East China University of Technology. Her research focuses on pattern recognition and the applications of machine learning in image processing.

Education: Ph.D., Nanjing University of Science and Technology.

Research: Image processing; pattern recognition; machine-learning methods.
