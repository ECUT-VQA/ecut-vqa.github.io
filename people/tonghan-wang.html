Associate Professor · Master's Supervisor · East China University of Technology

TW

About

Dr. Tonghan Wang is an associate professor and master’s supervisor at East China University of Technology. His research concerns AI applications in image processing, pattern recognition, and computer vision, including perceptual image quality assessment.

Education: Ph.D., Southeast University.

Research: Image quality assessment; AI for image processing; computer vision; pattern recognition.
