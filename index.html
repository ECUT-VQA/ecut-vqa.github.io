About Us

Visual Perception & Image Quality

ECUT-VQA is a research group at East China University of Technology. We investigate image quality assessment and perceptual visual computing, with an emphasis on effective, reproducible machine-learning methods.

ECUT-VQA 是东华理工大学视觉质量评价研究团队，关注图像质量评价、真实失真图像质量评估及计算机视觉相关研究。
